Help Desk & FAQ

Find quick answers to common questions about membership, loans, online applications, and cooperative services.

Frequently Asked Questions

Browse the most common support topics for TerraLink members and visitors.

01

How do I become a member?

Submit a valid ID, fill out the membership form, and visit the branch office for verification and orientation.

02

What are the basic membership requirements?

Members must be at least 18 years old, submit one government-issued ID, and complete the application form.

03

How long does application processing take?

Most membership applications are processed within 3 to 5 business days after documents are submitted.

04

Can I apply for a loan online?

Yes. You may start the application online, then visit the branch if additional verification is needed.

05

Where can I ask about my account?

You can contact the help desk by phone, email, or by visiting the cooperative office during business hours.

06

What services are available for members?

TerraLink offers savings, loan services, investment options, and community retail support through CoopMart.
