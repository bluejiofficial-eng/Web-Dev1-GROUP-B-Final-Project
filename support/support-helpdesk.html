Frequently Asked Questions

Browse the most common support topics for TerraLink members and applicants.

How do I apply for membership?

Complete the membership form, prepare one valid ID, and submit your details at the service desk for verification. New applicants may also request a short orientation schedule before final approval.

What documents are required?

For most requests, members only need a government-issued ID, an application form, and a recent 2x2 photo. Some loan-related concerns may require additional income documents.

How long is processing time?

Standard requests are processed within 3 to 5 business days, depending on the completeness of the submitted documents and the number of active applications in the queue.

Can I check loan options online?

Yes. Members may review loan estimates online using the calculator page before visiting the branch for final review. This helps applicants compare monthly payments and choose the right option.

What if I submitted an incomplete form?

The support team will contact you and explain which details need to be corrected or resubmitted. You can return the updated form to the branch or send it by email if the request is still open.

When is the help desk available?

The help desk is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, excluding holidays. For quick questions, the hotline and email support channels are also available during office hours.
