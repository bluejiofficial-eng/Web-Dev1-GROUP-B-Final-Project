Help Desk & FAQ

Find quick answers to common questions about membership, loans, online applications, and cooperative services.

Frequently Asked Questions

Browse the most common support topics for TerraLink members and applicants.

01

How do I apply for membership?

Complete the membership form, prepare one valid ID, and submit your details at the service desk for verification.

02

What documents are required?

For most requests, members only need a government-issued ID, an application form, and a recent 2x2 photo.

03

How long is processing time?

Standard requests are processed within 3 to 5 business days, depending on the completeness of the submitted documents.

04

Can I check loan options online?

Yes. Members may review loan estimates online using the calculator page before visiting the branch for final review.

05

What if I submitted an incomplete form?

The support team will contact you and explain which details need to be corrected or resubmitted.

06

When is the help desk available?

The help desk is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, excluding holidays.
